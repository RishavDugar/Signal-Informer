## Method & caveats

- **Events**: Claude with web search, 9 slices (3 years × Supreme Court / High Courts & arbitration / tribunals & regulators), told to include rulings with no stock move; then a second per-ruling research pass for what was public beforehand. Deduped by company ± 3 days.
- **Unusual move**: |abnormal return| ≥ max(3%, 2.5 × the stock's own daily abnormal σ over the prior 60 sessions) on T0, or the 3-day equivalent with most of it after T0.
- **Supreme Court replay is mechanical**: the real daily + advance schedule PDFs for the ruling day (still public on api.sci.gov.in) are searched with the live scanner's matcher, by case number, company and court-recorded party names. A name hitting > 4 items that day is too generic and only counts inside the verdict list.
- **Other forums**: old schedules aren't online, so 'date known' rests on news evidence.
- **On radar** = dispute public ≥ 7 days earlier. The live tracker only scans F&O stocks and needs its weekly discovery to have found the case, so this is an upper bound for level 1-2.
- **Bias**: an LLM-compiled event list over-represents rulings that made news. Rulings nobody reported are absent — they are also the ones least likely to move a stock.
